About ECOSOLE

Fresh shoes.
Less water.

ECOSOLE is a waterless shoe cleaning foam made for people who want a cleaner, simpler way to care for their favourite pairs. The rich foam lifts everyday dirt and marks with less mess, so you can refresh shoes at home, at work, or on the go without soaking them.

Waterless shoe care

Our 150 ml shoe cleaning foam is designed for low-water cleaning. Apply the foam, work it in with the built-in brush, and wipe away the residue with the included microfiber cloth.

Made for everyday shoes

ECOSOLE helps clean common dirt and daily build-up from sneakers and other suitable shoe surfaces. Always test a small hidden area first and follow the care guidance for your footwear.

0% water. 100% clean.

Our simple promise is convenience: a fresh-looking finish without carrying a bucket or making a mess. Keep ECOSOLE close for quick shoe cleaning whenever your rotation needs a reset.
